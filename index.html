Welcome to Severed Fates

Severed Fates is a tabletop role-playing game with a heavy focus on player agency. Visit the Compendium to view our full collection of guides, rules, and in game content. Have a look at the Tapestries page as well, where you're able to view and create characters, campaigns, and homebrew items. Finally, the Tools page contains a number of tools that will be helpful to you in your adventures, whether you are a Weaver or one of the Severed. Explore the companion, start your adventure, and Become Unbound.
